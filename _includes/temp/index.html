Google Workspace Migration Verification

Relationship between objectivelaw.org and temp.objectivelaw.org

The Objective Law Initiative owns and operates both objectivelaw.org and temp.objectivelaw.org.

temp.objectivelaw.org is a temporary Google Workspace migration domain. It is not a separate organization and is not intended to replace The Objective Law Initiative's permanent public website.

Why the temporary domain exists

The Objective Law Initiative is in the process of migrating its Google Workspace environment to a separate destination tenant. The organization's permanent production domain, objectivelaw.org, remains attached to the source Google Workspace tenant until the final migration cutover and therefore cannot yet be attached to the destination tenant.

For that reason, temp.objectivelaw.org was created specifically to serve as the destination Google Workspace tenant's temporary primary domain while data is migrated.

After the migration is completed, objectivelaw.org is intended to become the primary domain of the destination Google Workspace tenant, and temp.objectivelaw.org will no longer serve as the organization's primary Workspace domain.

Mission

The Objective Law Initiative fights to secure individual rights against arbitrary government power by developing and applying a constitutional framework grounded in rational principles of rights and limited government.

Additional information about the organization, its mission, programs, and activities is available on The Objective Law Initiative's About page.
