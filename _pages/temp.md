## Mission

The Objective Law Initiative fights to secure individual rights against arbitrary government power by developing and applying a constitutional framework grounded in rational principles of rights and limited government.

</section>

<section class="oli-section" markdown="1">

## Programs and activities

The Objective Law Initiative is a nonprofit public-interest legal and educational organization. Its work includes:

- legal, historical, and philosophical research concerning constitutional interpretation, individual rights, objective law, and limits on government power
- strategic amicus curiae briefs and other public-interest legal advocacy
- educational publications, legal explainers, commentary, newsletters, videos, podcasts, and related materials
- public education and collaboration with attorneys, scholars, students, educational institutions, and nonprofit organizations

The Objective Law Initiative's present legal advocacy is principally strategic amicus curiae work and related research and education. The organization does not itself engage in direct litigation or client representation.

Learn more about [why the organization exists](/about/#why-oli-exists), its [activities](/about/#activities), current [resources](/resources/), and ways to [contribute or collaborate](/contribute/).

</section>

<section class="oli-section" markdown="1">

## Why the temporary domain exists

The Objective Law Initiative is migrating Google Workspace to a separate destination tenant. The organization's permanent production domain, **objectivelaw.org**, remains attached to the source Google Workspace tenant until final cutover. Because a domain cannot be attached to both tenants at once, objectivelaw.org cannot yet be attached to the destination tenant.

The Objective Law Initiative created **temp.objectivelaw.org** specifically to serve as the destination Google Workspace tenant's temporary primary domain while the migration is in progress. This temporary domain belongs to the same organization and does not represent a separate entity or program.

After the migration, **objectivelaw.org** is intended to become the destination tenant's primary domain. temp.objectivelaw.org will then cease serving as the organization's primary Workspace domain.

</section>
